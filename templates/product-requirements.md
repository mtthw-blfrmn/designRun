## Data and service contract

Specify data ownership, validation, persistence, idempotency, concurrency, authorization, deletion/retention, failure semantics, compatibility, and migration implications where relevant.

## Interface quality constraints

Record content, accessibility, responsive, localization, motion, loading, offline, privacy, security, performance, and analytics requirements that materially affect behavior.

## Success and guardrails

Define the outcome signal, instrumentation, baseline, target or decision threshold, measurement window, and guardrail metrics. Mark synthetic assumptions.

## Acceptance and verification

Trace each release gate to requirement IDs. Include unit, integration, end-to-end, visual, accessibility, security/privacy, resilience, and manual checks proportional to risk.

## Dependencies, risks, assumptions, and open questions

Name owner and resolution condition for anything that can block or materially change scope.
