# Code of conduct

designRun contributors should make participation safe, constructive, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and open to correction.
- Critique ideas and artifacts without attacking people.
- Welcome contributors with different disciplines, backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience.
- Protect private information and respect the rights attached to research and reference material.
- Give credit and represent evidence honestly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed in good faith with attention to safety, privacy, context, and proportional response.

Project maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors whose behavior is incompatible with this standard.
