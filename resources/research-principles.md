# Research principles

Research reduces uncertainty; it does not manufacture certainty.

## Practice

- Begin with the decision the evidence should inform.
- Preserve the source, date, participant or population, and limitations.
- Separate observation, interpretation, and product implication.
- Record contradictions and missing perspectives.
- Avoid converting frequency in a small sample into prevalence.
- Protect private and customer-confidential material.
- Connect stable findings to project decisions and requirements.

An agent should cite the actual source path or URL and state when it cannot inspect the source directly.
