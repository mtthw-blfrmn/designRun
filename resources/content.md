# Content

Product language should make objects, actions, state, and consequences concrete.

## Rules

- Use the user's vocabulary and literal product nouns.
- Label actions with verbs that predict the result.
- Put critical information at the decision point.
- Explain errors with cause and recovery.
- Use examples and defaults to create momentum.
- Keep helper text for information the interface cannot express structurally.
- Avoid promotional language inside operational tools.

When terminology is a product decision, record it in the project decision log rather than letting screens drift.
