# Linear minimal product hero

## Observed behavior

Large typography, restrained navigation, a dark field, and one high-detail product surface form a focused product story. Complexity is concentrated in the inspectable product preview while the frame remains quiet.

## What works

Minimalism feels intentional because hierarchy, spacing, contrast, and mockup detail are all precise. The product—not generic atmosphere—provides credibility.

## Principle to inherit

Let the actual product surface carry richness. Surround it with only enough structure to establish hierarchy and meaning.

## Do not copy literally

Do not copy Linear’s brand, messaging, issue-tracker UI, hero composition, or turn an operational product into a landing page.
