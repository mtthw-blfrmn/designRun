# Cluely functional product mockup

## Observed behavior

A compact guided-action layer sits over the moment where assistance is useful. The staged desktop context creates scale and atmosphere while a small set of prompts and follow-up actions prove the product can act.

## What works

The image presents one desirable, inspectable product moment instead of a montage. Intelligence is communicated through contextual actions, not generic AI decoration.

## Principle to inherit

A product mockup should stage one believable use moment and include only the controls needed to demonstrate that the product works.

## Do not copy literally

Do not reuse Cluely’s brand, positioning, meeting content, translucent treatment, or convert an unrelated product into an overlay tool.
