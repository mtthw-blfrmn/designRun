# Fey product polish

## Observed behavior

Dense settings and billing surfaces use layered near-black surfaces, precise separators, compact controls, and carefully tuned muted text. A simple product hero uses scale and one product object rather than decoration.

## What works

No single flourish carries the interface. Quality accumulates through grouping, contrast, icon alignment, label hierarchy, state treatment, and spacing across utility screens.

## Principle to inherit

World-class polish is a systems property: tune every state and relationship, and treat utility screens as first-class product surfaces.

## Do not copy literally

Do not reproduce Fey’s finance aesthetic, brand, dock, payment visuals, or use low-contrast dark UI without rigorous accessibility verification.
