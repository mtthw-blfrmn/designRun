# ElevenLabs media explore workspace

## Observed behavior

Persistent navigation, page tabs, search, a rich masonry grid, prompt chips, and a floating creation instrument coexist in a light interface. Content provides visual energy while dividers, labels, and controls stay restrained.

## What works

Browsing and creation remain simultaneously available without competing. Dense information is organized through hierarchy and state precision instead of heavy containers.

## Principle to inherit

Keep product chrome quiet and complete: let inspectable content carry energy, and keep the primary creation instrument stable and close to the work.

## Do not copy literally

Do not reproduce ElevenLabs branding, navigation, media taxonomy, generated examples, or masonry layout when the content model does not benefit from it.

## Verification notes

Product surfaces evolve. Verify the current [ElevenLabs Image & Video experience](https://elevenlabs.io/image-video) before using detailed behavior as evidence.
