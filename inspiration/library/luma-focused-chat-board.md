# Luma focused chat board

## Observed behavior

A narrow centered conversation floats above a stable, wide composer. Minimal navigation preserves orientation while preview content and a dark ambient background add depth.

## What works

The screen behaves like a focused generative workbench rather than a messaging feed. The composer feels like an instrument: grounded, polished, and continuously available.

## Principle to inherit

In generative flows, center the active conversation, stabilize the primary composer, and minimize chrome; supporting atmosphere must deepen focus, not become the interface.

## Do not copy literally

Do not reuse Luma branding, prompt examples, exact navigation, or dark treatment as a generic AI aesthetic.
