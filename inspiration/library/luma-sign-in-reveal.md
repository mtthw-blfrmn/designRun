# Luma Dream Machine sign-in reveal

## Observed behavior

One centered authentication task dominates the screen. Brand, stacked actions, and a small product artifact reveal form a strict hierarchy against an atmospheric background.

## What works

The screen has one job. Atmosphere and a preview of the product world intensify that job without turning authentication into a marketing page.

## Principle to inherit

On single-purpose screens, use hierarchy, polish, and a small product reveal to strengthen the primary action; remove every competing task.

## Do not copy literally

Do not default to Luma’s gradient language, auth providers, image stack, or let atmosphere substitute for usable structure.
