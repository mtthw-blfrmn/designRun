# Sana product-card composition

## Observed behavior

Large product cards combine short descriptions, clear actions, and rich product previews within a spacious editorial grid. Neutral surfaces dominate; vivid product content and small accents provide energy.

## What works

Major product areas are easy to compare because they receive enough space to feel substantial and inspectable. The previews carry complexity while the card structure stays simple.

## Principle to inherit

Use large cards only for major objects or choices. Pair restrained structure with a specific, information-bearing preview and a small decisive accent.

## Do not copy literally

Do not reuse Sana branding, typography, neon accents, or promote every piece of information into an oversized rounded card.
