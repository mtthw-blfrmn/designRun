# Giga photographic product mockup

## Observed behavior

Natural photography creates emotional contrast behind a believable dark product surface. Product panels remain legible enough to explain objects, policies, scenarios, and rules while environment and depth add presence.

## What works

The mockup feels grounded and specific rather than like a generic dashboard pasted into a frame. Atmosphere supports the product surface without hiding it.

## Principle to inherit

Use photography only when it reinforces the product story. Product mockups must remain believable, legible, and specific enough to communicate capability.

## Do not copy literally

Do not reuse Giga’s branding, landscapes, agent-canvas design, or add unrelated nature imagery merely to signal premium quality.
