# Image direction

Treat image work as product communication, not decoration. Inspect the target surface, message, composition constraints, supplied images, and relevant references before generating or editing.

## Establish the job

Identify:

- the audience, placement, crop, and required output dimensions
- the single product idea the image must make legible
- visual hierarchy, focal subject, environment, lighting, material, camera, and negative-space needs
- exact product UI, copy, logo, or device geometry that must remain faithful
- references to inherit from and the specific quality each contributes
- legal, privacy, likeness, brand, and redistribution constraints

Do not copy a reference composition literally. Extract portable principles such as contrast, framing, density, surface treatment, or motion.

## Choose the medium

- Use the harness's image-generation or image-editing capability for raster scenes, photography, illustration, texture, and composited mockups.
- Use code, SVG, or a native design tool for diagrams, icons, logos, simple geometric graphics, and UI that must stay precisely editable.
- For a product mockup, keep the interface inspectable. Generate the environment separately when generation would distort text or UI, then composite the verified product surface using an appropriate available tool.
- If the needed capability is unavailable, produce a precise art-direction brief and asset plan rather than pretending an image was created.

## Iterate with intent

Create one strong direction first. Use variants only to test a meaningful choice such as framing, material, product prominence, or emotional tone. Evaluate each result at its actual placement size for legibility, crop resilience, unwanted artifacts, false product details, and contrast with surrounding content.

For edits, preserve every element the user did not ask to change. Reinspect the result after each material edit instead of relying on the prompt alone.

## Save and record

Save redistributable global material in the appropriate `resources/` asset area and project-specific material in the owning project or deliverable. Preserve an asset manifest containing source or prompt, tool/model and material settings when useful, creation date, license/provenance, pixel dimensions, aspect ratio, color profile or delivery color space, alpha behavior, intended crop/placements, responsive focal point, compression/export format, and known limitations.

Inspect at 100% and at actual placement size. Check hands, faces, type, logos, reflections, repeated texture, geometry, edge halos, banding, crop safety, contrast under overlays, and false product claims. Strip sensitive metadata when required, but preserve provenance in the manifest. Do not upload, publish, or place private source imagery into a public artifact without explicit authorization.
