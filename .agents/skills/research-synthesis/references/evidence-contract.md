# Evidence contract

Assign stable source IDs and finding IDs when the synthesis may inform requirements or decisions.

## Source record

Record provenance, date, evidence type, collection method, participant/context, represented segment, consent/privacy boundary, and limitations. Preserve exact quotations only when necessary and permitted; otherwise use a faithful paraphrase linked to the source.

## Finding record

- Claim: one falsifiable or bounded statement.
- Evidence: source IDs and the observations that support it.
- Counterevidence: contradiction, exception, or absent segment.
- Confidence: high, medium, or low with a reason—not a cosmetic score.
- Scope: users, situations, and time period to which it may apply.
- Implication: a product question or constraint, not an automatic feature.
- Next test: the smallest uncertainty-reducing step.

Do not convert frequency in a convenience sample into prevalence. Do not infer intent from behavior without corroboration. Do not combine market evidence, usability evidence, stakeholder preference, and telemetry as though they establish the same thing.
